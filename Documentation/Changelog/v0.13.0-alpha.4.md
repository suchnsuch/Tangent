+ Added the ability to [[Queries|Query]] for Sections, e.g. `Sections with #Tag`. Card, List, and Feed Lenses have all been updated to support displaying these sub-note elements. Card & Feed Lenses show just the section that was found rather than the entire note. // [Issue 471](https://github.com/suchnsuch/Tangent/issues/471)

+ Added alerts for copying paths and sections. // PRs [476](https://github.com/suchnsuch/Tangent/issues/476), [480](https://github.com/suchnsuch/Tangent/issues/480), & [484](https://github.com/suchnsuch/Tangent/issues/484)
+ Updated Electron to v45.0.0 (among other dependencies).
